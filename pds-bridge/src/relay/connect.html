Checking your sign-in…

Read this before you click. This is a second, separate authorization — signing in proved you own the handle; this grants posting access to the account behind it.

  • What the bridge will be able to do, at the protocol level: Bluesky's OAuth has no "only create posts" grant yet, so the permission you approve (transition:generic) means write access to your repo generally.
  • What it will actually do: create app.bsky.feed.post records, plus two me.browserid.* provenance records per post — and only when a live, unrevoked warrant and a valid agent signature say so. No deletes, edits, likes, follows, or profile changes.
  • Those provenance records are permanent, public, and visible on the firehose. They live in your repo. They are yours — you can delete them; the bridge will not.
  • How to stop it: revoke the warrant. That is checked before any post is written, so revoking stops posting immediately. You can also disconnect this app at your own PDS, which is your only recourse if the bridge itself is ever compromised.
